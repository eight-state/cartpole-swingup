HOST SENSITIVITY

Byte and hash checks are portable. Numerical replay can depend on the operating system, Python build, numerical libraries, and processor behavior.

Quoted floating-point values are release-host observations unless a capsule states otherwise. Trailing digits are not treated as portable evidence.

The registry runs each capsule with its own lockfile and verification command. It does not weaken tolerances, suppress failures, or claim that a passing authority audit certifies a host-sensitive trajectory.

n=5 through n=11 use their published Linux CI boundary. n=12 through n=14 use their published Windows CI boundary. n=14 CI checks authority without claiming that the hosted runner certifies its retained numerical trajectory.
