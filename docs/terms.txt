TERMS

Success
  A rung's sampled predicate passed. The predicate, hold metric, force bound, and track bound come from rungs.toml.

Proof
  One deterministic run passed a fixed verifier. It does not mean a mathematical proof of stability, robustness, or reachability.

Historical evidence
  Frozen records from an earlier run. The shared verifier checks bytes, hashes, arithmetic, and internal consistency without rerunning the original perturbation study.

Fresh replay
  A new simulation using the shared package, root lockfile, retained numerical assets, and the rung's configured adapter.

Exact
  The word is qualified where used. It can describe byte-identical data, exact zero-order-hold discretization, or an exact equilibrium start.

Reproducible
  The documented command reproduces a stated check within its dependency and numerical-host boundary. Trailing floating-point digits are release-host values unless evidence establishes more.
